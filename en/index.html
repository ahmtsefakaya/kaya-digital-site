FAQ

Questions, answered

How much does a website cost?

Setup is a one-time $450 — or $299 with founding client pricing for our first 5 clients. Plans then start at $39/month and include hosting, domain, SSL, security updates and small edits. You see a free preview before paying anything.

How fast can my site be ready?

We usually build your free preview within 48 hours of getting your details. Once you approve it, going live typically takes a day or two, depending on your domain.

What do I need to provide?

Just the basics: your business name, services, service area, hours and how customers should reach you. Photos and a logo help but aren’t required — we work with what you have.

Do I own my website?

Yes. Your domain is registered in your name and your content is yours. If you ever leave, we hand over your files and domain.

What if I want changes later?

Text or email us. Small edits are included in every plan, and we handle changes within 2 business days. One-off changes outside your plan are $35.

Do you build Spanish websites?

Sí. We can add a Spanish version of your site ($199, included in Pro), and we’re happy to work with you in English, Spanish or Turkish.

Do you work with businesses outside the US?

Yes. We work remotely with businesses across the United States, Europe and beyond. Everything happens by email, text or WhatsApp — in English, Spanish or Turkish. Prices are in USD.

Do I need to get on a phone call?

No. We work entirely by text and email, so you can reply whenever it suits you — between jobs, after closing, whenever.

Can I keep my current domain?

Yes. If you already own a domain, we connect your new site to it. If you don’t have one, we register one in your name.

What is “automation”, in plain English?

Small tools that do routine work for you: instantly replying to new inquiries, sending appointment reminders, asking happy customers for a Google review, or answering common questions with a chat assistant. Set it up once; it works every day.

How do I cancel?

Monthly plans have no contract. Give us 30 days’ notice by text or email and you’re done. We’ll hand over your files and domain.
