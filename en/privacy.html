Privacy note

Last updated: October 2026

What we collect

Only what you type into our contact form — your name, business name, business type, email, optional phone number, language preference and message — or what you send us by email.

How we use it

Only to reply to you and to prepare your website preview or quote. We never sell, rent or share your information for marketing.

How the form is delivered

Our contact form is delivered by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), which forwards your message to our inbox.

Visit statistics

We use a simple visit counter to see which pages are viewed (page, referrer and approximate city). We do not use advertising cookies or sell this data.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete the information you sent us. Just email us and we’ll take care of it.
